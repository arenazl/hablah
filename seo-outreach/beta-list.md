## What Makes It Unique

1. **Speak-first, not lesson-first.** Most apps gate speaking behind weeks of curriculum. Habláh opens with conversation on day one and scaffolds vocabulary around what you actually try to say.

2. **Real-time bidirectional streaming.** Sub-1-second latency. The coach interrupts, reacts, and laughs at the right times. It feels like a person, not a turn-based chatbot.

3. **Correction priority engine.** The coach doesn't nitpick every mistake. It surfaces errors that block fluency (verb tense confusion, fossilized pronunciation) and lets minor stuff slide — same way a human tutor would.

4. **Push-to-speak coaching.** If you go quiet, the coach asks. If you answer in monosyllables, it asks you to expand. The training prompts are tuned against thousands of real sessions to detect under-engagement.

5. **Accent + persona selection.** Choose your tutor's accent (rioplatense, neutral LATAM, British, American, Australian) and personality (patient, demanding, casual). Same content, very different vibe.

6. **No certificate theater.** No streaks, no badges, no XP. Progress is measured in minutes spoken and corrections internalized.
